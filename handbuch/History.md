# History

Holdout records every change to the stock: what was added, eaten, thrown away or corrected, how many packages, **who** did it and **when**. The person is the one chosen on this device under ["Who are you?"](Family.md).

## Taking packages out

When you use something from the supply, take it out of its [batch](Batches.md) so the stock stays right:

1. Tap the **minus** icon of the batch: in the **Batches** list, in the **Batches** section of a product page, or **Take out** at the top of a batch's page.
2. Enter how many **packages** (1 up to what the batch still has).
3. Pick the **reason**: **Consumed** (eaten or drunk) or **Discarded** (thrown away, e.g. spoiled).
4. Optionally add a **note** (up to 150 characters), e.g. "for the soup".

The batch gets smaller. When it is empty it disappears from the lists; its history stays.

If two people take from the same batch at the same moment and there is not enough left, the second one gets a message with what is actually left and nothing is changed.

## What is recorded

| Entry | When | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Added | a batch is added (by hand or after scanning) | `+` its packages |
| Consumed | packages are taken out because they were used | `−` packages |
| Discarded | packages are taken out because they were thrown away | `−` packages |
| Correction | the number of packages of a batch is changed by hand, or a batch is deleted | the difference, or `−` all its packages |

Changing only the date, location or note of a batch records nothing.

## The History page

- Newest entries first. On a phone, each row shows the product with the change, the person and the note underneath (e.g. `−2 × 500 g · Alex · for the soup`); on the right the kind of entry with date and time.
- Search finds the product name, brand and barcode. The filters narrow the list to one kind of entry, one person or one product.
- The history cannot be edited or deleted by hand. To fix a wrong amount, change the batch; the correction appears as its own entry.

On a product's page, the **History** section shows the latest entries for that product.

## When things are removed

- **A batch is deleted or emptied:** its entries stay.
- **A person is removed** from the family list: their entries stay and show **Unknown** as the person.
- **A product is deleted:** its batches and its history are deleted with it.
