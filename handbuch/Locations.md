# Locations

A **location** is a place where you keep part of your supply, for example "Cellar shelf", "Kitchen cupboard" or "Garage". Every [batch](Batches.md) can name one, so in an emergency everyone knows where to look.

Locations are optional. Batches without a location still count towards the supply.

## Managing locations

Open **Locations** in the menu. Everything happens in dialogs on this page:

- **Add location** asks for a name (required, up to 100 characters, unique within the household) and an optional note (up to 150 characters), e.g. "left shelf, top".
- Tap a row or the pencil to rename a location or change its note.
- The bin deletes a location. Its batches stay in the supply, without a location.

The list shows how many packages are stored at each location. Tap the number to open the [Batches](Batches.md) page filtered to that location.

## Choosing a location for a batch

When you add or edit a batch, pick the **Location**. If it does not exist yet, the **+** button next to the field adds it on the spot, without leaving the form.

## Where locations appear

- On the **Batches** page, each row shows amount, location and note under the product name, e.g. `4 × 500 g · Cellar shelf · on offer`. The **Location** filter shows only the batches at one place.
- In the **Batches** section of a product page, the location is shown under the amount.
