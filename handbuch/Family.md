# Family

One installation serves one household. Everyone in the household works on the same supply, each with their own account. Nobody has a password: people sign in with a passkey, an email link or an authenticator app (see [Sign-in and security](Sign-in-and-security.md)).

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** (German: Admin) | Everything members can, plus invite people, resend or revoke invitations, and remove members |
| **Member** | Use the app and see who belongs to the household |

The person who creates the household becomes its owner. Owners cannot remove themselves.

## Setting up the household

On a fresh installation, every page leads to **Set up your household**: enter a name for the household, your own name, your email address and how many adults and children live in the household (see [Household](Household.md)). You receive a sign-in link by email. Afterwards, registration is closed; everyone else joins by invitation.

## Inviting someone

1. Open **Family** and choose **Invite**.
2. Enter the email address and, optionally, the name.
3. The person receives an email with a link that is valid for 7 days.
4. They open the link, confirm their name and choose **Accept invitation**. They are signed in right away and Holdout suggests setting up a passkey.

- Each invitation link works once.
- Inviting the same address again sends a new link and restarts the 7 days.
- An address that already has an account cannot be invited.

Owners see pending invitations under **Invitations**, with their expiry date, and can **Resend** or **Revoke** them there.

## Removing a member

Owners can remove a member on the **Family** page. The member's account, passkeys and authenticator-app setup are deleted; their entries in the history stay, without the name.

## Profile and language

Open the menu at the top right and choose **Profile** to change your name and the **language** of the app, and to manage how you sign in (passkeys, authenticator app; see [Sign-in and security](Sign-in-and-security.md)). Every person chooses their own language.

Available languages: English (the main language), Deutsch, Español, Português and Français. Until a person chooses one, the app uses the browser's language if it is one of these, otherwise English. The sign-in pages always follow the browser's language.
