# Family

One installation serves one household. Everyone in the household works on the same supply.

## No sign-in

Holdout runs on your home network and has no accounts, passwords or sign-in. Anyone who can reach it on your network can use it, so never make it reachable from the internet.

So that the history shows who used or bought something, each device asks once **"Who are you?"** and remembers the answer for a long time (also after closing the browser or restarting the server).

- Tap your name to continue.
- Not in the list yet? Choose **Not in the list? Add yourself**, enter your name and continue.
- To change who is using a device, open the menu at the top right and choose **Switch person**.

## Setting up the household

On a fresh installation, every page leads to **Set up your household**: enter a name for the household and your own name. Afterwards, setup is closed; everyone else adds themselves on the "Who are you?" page, or someone adds them on the Family page.

## The Family page

Under **Family**, everyone can:

- **Add person:** enter a name.
- **Edit:** change a name.
- **Remove:** delete a person. The person currently using this device cannot remove themselves; switch person first.
