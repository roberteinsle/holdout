# Equipment

Food and water are only part of being prepared. The **Equipment** page keeps everything else: the home pharmacy, what you need in a power outage, hygiene articles, documents, cash, the grab bag, fire safety and how you stay informed, plus any other equipment such as dishes or tools.

## Areas

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Home pharmacy | personal medication, skin and wound disinfectant, cold remedies, fever thermometer, anti-diarrhoea medication, insect bite and sunburn ointment, splinter tweezers, bandages, first aid kit |
| Power outage | battery or crank radio, torch, spare batteries, power bank, candles, tea lights, matches or lighter, camping stove with fuel, warm blankets, sleeping bags |
| Hygiene | soap, toothpaste, toilet paper, rubbish bags, disinfectant, household gloves, emergency toilet bucket |
| Documents | waterproof document folder with identity cards, birth and marriage certificates, insurance policies, certificates, will, lasting power of attorney (originals or certified copies) |
| Cash | cash in small notes and coins: card payment and cash machines do not work without power |
| Grab bag | a backpack for an evacuation with medication, document copies, clothing, torch, radio, sleeping bag, water, head covering, helmet, protective mask, makeshift respiratory protection, work gloves |
| Fire safety | smoke detectors, fire extinguisher, fire blanket |
| Staying informed | a warning app such as NINA, emergency numbers on paper |
| Other equipment | anything else, e.g. dishes and cutlery |

The suggestions follow the guidance of the German Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance (BBK), [Vorsorgen für Krisen und Katastrophen](https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Vorsorge/vorsorge_node.html).

## Starting from the checklist

Choose **Add suggestions**, pick the areas and confirm. The suggested items are added as **missing**, named in your language and with a sensible check interval (for example power bank every 3 months, fire extinguisher every 24 months). Tick them off as you get them: open an item and switch on **Available**, or use **Check**. Running it again adds only what is not there yet, also if you renamed an item; items you deleted come back.

## An item

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Name, Area | What it is and where it belongs |
| Available | Whether the household has it. Missing items stay on the list as a to-do. |
| Quantity, Location | Optional, e.g. "2" and "Cellar shelf" |
| Expires on | Optional, e.g. the gas cartridge of the camping stove, medication, the service date of the fire extinguisher |
| Check every … months | Optional. After this time since the last check, the item shows **Check due**. |
| Note | Anything else |

## Status

Every item shows one status, the most urgent one, always with an icon and a label:

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| **Missing** | not available |
| **Expired** | the expiry date has passed |
| **Needs attention** | the last check found a problem |
| **Check due** | the check interval has passed since the last check |
| **Expires soon** | it expires within 60 days |
| **OK** | none of the above |

## Checking and the check log

Tap the check icon next to an item (or **Check** on its page) and choose the result:

- **OK, present and working**: the item counts as available; the next check is due after the interval.
- **Needs attention**, e.g. "batteries empty": the item keeps this status until a later check says OK or replaced.
- **Replaced or refilled**: available again; enter the new expiry date, e.g. of a new gas cartridge.

Add a note if you like. Every check is kept in the item's **Check log** with date, person, result and note. To confirm several items at once, select them and choose **Checked: all OK**.

## The list

Items are grouped by area in the order above, alphabetically within each area. Search finds name, location and note; filters narrow the list to an area or a status, e.g. everything that is **Missing** or **Check due**. On a phone, quantity and location appear under the name and the check is a single tap.

## On paper

The [printout](Printout.md) contains the **Equipment checklist**: all items by area, ☒ for available and ☐ for missing, with expiry date, last check, any status that needs action, and an empty **Checked** column for ticking items off by hand. Phones keep it in their [offline copy](Offline.md) as well.
